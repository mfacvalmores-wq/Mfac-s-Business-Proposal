Introduction of the Business

Welcome to Choco Latte, a cozy neighborhood sanctuary where the rich, comforting embrace of artisanal chocolate meets the smooth, invigorating craft of specialty coffee. Nestled in the heart of the community, our cafe is designed as a warm haven for workers who are in a rush, and friends looking to slow down and savor the moment.

Target Market

Age Range & Audience: Our target market comprises young professionals, early-career managers, and busy parents aged 25 to 45 who balance demanding careers and families.

Location: We are positioned across urban and semi-urban business districts, near corporate parks, co-working spaces, transit hubs, and major commercial centers throughout the Philippines.

Lifestyle & Purchasing Behavior: Our customers maintain fast-paced schedules featuring early commutes, long desk hours, and frequent overtime that lead to chronic mental and physical fatigue. To cope, they rely on quick, accessible caffeine sources—such as instant coffee, cafe runs, or ready-to-drink options—to jumpstart their mornings and beat the afternoon slump.

Food Preferences & Suitability: They are convenience-driven, productivity-focused individuals drawn to small luxuries and comfort foods like sweet chocolate-coffee blends that offer a rapid mental break or mood boost during stressful workdays.

Business Location

Proposed Location: The proposed location of our cafe business is along Don Apolinar Velez Street, Cagayan de Oro City, Misamis Oriental, situated in the heart of the downtown commercial and business district of the city.

Reason for Choosing the Location: Don Apolinar Velez Street was chosen because it is one of the main and busiest streets in downtown CDO, functioning as a major city thoroughfare with very high daily traffic. Lined with companies, schools, and commercial buildings, it ensures a continuous flow of potential customers looking for a place to dine, study, work, and unwind.

Accessibility: The location is highly accessible to everyone, being serviced by almost all public jeepney routes going to Divisoria, Carmen, and Lapasan. It is accessible to private vehicles with nearby pay-parking areas, features wide and pedestrian-friendly sidewalks for walk-in customers, and remains easily accessible for suppliers and delivery riders like GrabFood and Foodpanda.

Nearby Establishments & Landmarks: The cafe is surrounded by key establishments such as banks and BPO/office buildings; schools and universities like City Central School and MOGCHS; malls and commercial centers including Centrio Mall, Gaisano, and local shops; hotels, hospitals, and government offices; and prominent landmarks like Gaston Park, St. Augustine Cathedral, and Plaza Divisoria.

Potential Customers in the Area: Our main target customers include students and young professionals studying or reviewing in the area, office workers and employees from nearby companies, banks, and BPOs looking for coffee breaks and lunch meetings, as well as shoppers and commuters passing through Divisoria and downtown.

Advantages of the Location: Being on a main downtown street guarantees high daily visibility to thousands of people. It provides a stable daily customer base from nearby offices and schools, answers the high demand for a comfortable, air-conditioned venue for meetings, studying, and casual hangouts, and increases the potential for bulk orders and catering for corporate events.
